About

Everyone deserves to be heard before they're judged.

A resume shows where someone has been. A conversation shows what they can do. We make that conversation possible for every applicant.

What we believe

  • Same chance for everyone

    Every candidate for a role is scored on the same rules.

  • Evidence you can check

    Every score links back to what was really said.

  • People make the decision

    AI prepares the evidence. Your team makes the hiring call.
