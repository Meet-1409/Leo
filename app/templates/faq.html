FAQ

Common questions

For hiring teams

  • Are the questions the same for everyone?

    They come from the same job description and rules, and follow-ups depend on each answer.

  • Does the AI decide who gets hired?

    No. It prepares a report, and your team makes the decision.

  • How do credits work?

    Credits are reserved per interview minute when you send an invitation.

For candidates

  • What do I need?

    A laptop with a camera and microphone, a current browser, and a quiet room.

  • Am I being recorded?

    Yes. You will be told before joining, and asked to agree.

  • What if my connection drops?

    If the call never connected, try again from the same link. If a call drops after it has started, open the same link within 5 minutes to rejoin. The timer keeps running.
