FAQ

Common questions

For hiring teams

  • Are the questions the same for everyone?

    They come from the same job description and rules, and follow-ups depend on each answer.

  • Does the AI decide who gets hired?

    No. It prepares a report, and your team makes the decision.

  • How do credits work?

    An interview uses 5 credits a minute, reserved when you send an invitation. They are returned if the invitation is cancelled or expires unused.

  • How long does a candidate have?

    An invitation lasts 7 days. You can send it again or cancel it while it is waiting.

  • What does proctoring check?

    Whether the candidate's face is in view, whether anyone else is present, phones and similar objects, and leaving the tab or full screen. Repeated warnings end the interview, and every warning is listed in the report.

  • Can I watch the interview?

    Yes. The candidate's camera and microphone are recorded for job interviews, and the recording is kept for 30 days.

  • Who can see a report?

    Only members of your organization. Viewers can read reports, and recruiters, admins and owners can shortlist or reject.

For candidates

  • What do I need?

    A laptop with a camera and microphone, a current browser, and a quiet room.

  • Am I being recorded?

    Yes. You will be told before joining, and asked to agree.

  • What if my connection drops?

    If the call never connected, try again from the same link. If a call drops after it has started, open the same link within 5 minutes to rejoin. The timer keeps running.

  • Can I take an interview again?

    A job interview can be taken once. You can practice as often as you like.

  • Will I see my report?

    You see the report for practice interviews and certifications. A job interview's report goes to the employer.

  • What happens if I get a proctoring warning?

    You see the warning on screen and can fix the problem. Repeated warnings of the same kind end the interview.
