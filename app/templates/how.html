How it works

A proper interview, from one job description.

Set up the role once. Every candidate gets a live voice interview shaped by that role and their experience.

Before the call

  • Describe the role

    Paste your job description, or write it with AI help. Add skills, experience range, language and interview length.

  • Invite people

    Each candidate gets a private link by email. Credits are reserved when you send it.

  • Check their setup

    Camera, microphone and speakers are tested before anyone joins.

During the call

  • Questions from the job description

    Maya asks one question at a time and follows up on what the person actually says.

  • Live transcript

    The conversation appears on screen as it happens, so a missed word is not a lost question.

  • Proctoring

    Warnings appear if a face leaves the frame, someone else joins, a phone shows up, or the tab changes.

After the call

  • A report you can check

    Scores, the transcript, the recording, and each answer beside what a strong answer includes.

  • Compare candidates

    Everyone for a role is scored on the same rules, so you can shortlist with evidence.

  • People decide

    The report supports your hiring team. It never rejects anyone by itself.
