What went wrong?

Stuck on an interview, a login code or your credits? Tell us what happened. If you have an account, you can also log in and follow your request there.

Common fixes

  • No login code?

    Check your spam folder, then ask for a new code. Each code lasts 10 minutes.

  • Camera or microphone blocked?

    Allow both in your browser's site settings, then reload the page.

  • Can't hear the interviewer?

    Turn your volume up, then use Test sound on the device check.
