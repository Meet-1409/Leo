The AI interviewer

Meet Maya.

Maya holds a live voice interview built from your job description, then gives your team a report with evidence from what the candidate said.

Start hiringHow it works

In the interview

  • Questions from your job description

    Maya has no fixed question list. She asks about the role you are hiring for.

  • A real conversation

    She listens and asks follow-up questions based on each answer.

  • Proctored

    Camera and tab checks flag anything your team should review.

After the interview

  • The same rules for everyone

    Every candidate for a role is scored the same way.

  • Evidence you can check

    Each score links back to the transcript.

  • Your team decides

    Maya prepares the evidence. People make the hiring call.
